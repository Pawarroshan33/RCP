About Proprietor

CA Roshan Pawar, is a qualified Chartered Accountant and proprietor of M/s Roshan C Pawar & Co. Young, energetic and dynamic Roshan, established his firm in year 2022. With an aim to be leader in the field, he started his office at Nagpur, quickly established its associates in Mumbai, Pune and Gondia. His passion for the profession led him to begin preparing for examination way before his 12th itself. After completing his higher secondary, he got admitted to premier institute “Sydenham College of Commerce & Economics”, Mumbai wherefrom he secured his graduation with excellent grades. Simultaneously he got opportunity to be associated with M/s D V Kalk & Associates, Mumbai, wherein he completed his mandatory articleship of 3.5 years as well. After completing articleship, CA Roshan Pawar joined M/s. C Vidyarthi & Co., Chartered accountant in Nagpur; one of the top 10 CA firms in Nagpur. Wherein he worked for 7 years as senior auditor and tax advisor for Nagpur and Mumbai branch. CA Roshan Pawar, has gathered vast experience in the journey so far.
